What to expect on a first visit

日本基督教団 仙台宮城野教会 describes itself: “仙台宮城野教会仙台市宮城野区にあるキリスト教会です。.” Expect ancient liturgy engaging all the senses — chant, incense, and icons. Much of the congregation stands, and people come and go quietly during the long service; arriving mid-liturgy is normal and unremarkable.

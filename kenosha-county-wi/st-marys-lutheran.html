What to expect on a first visit

St. Marys Lutheran describes itself: “All are Welcome at St.” The service follows a printed liturgy with hymns, readings, a sermon, and often Communion. Everything you need to follow along is in the bulletin or on screen. Children's programming or nursery care is available. If you'd rather look before you visit, services are streamed online.

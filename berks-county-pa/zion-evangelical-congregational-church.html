What to expect on a first visit

Zion Evangelical Congregational Church describes itself: “To Know Christ & To Make Him Known.” The service centers on the sermon, with a relaxed structure around it. Music is led by a contemporary band. Children's programming or nursery care is available. If you'd rather look before you visit, services are streamed online.

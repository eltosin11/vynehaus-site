Hope Presbyterian Church - Crosspoint Ministry

26421 Pacific Highway South, Des Moines

What to expect on a first visit

Hope Presbyterian Church - Crosspoint Ministry describes itself: “말씀으로 시작하는 하루 인사말, 비전, 섬기는 이들 주일예배, 성경공부, 문화 및 한글학교 주소, 연락처, 구글맵 모바일 헌금 및 안내.” The service is orderly and word-centered: hymns, prayers, scripture, and a thoughtful sermon at its heart. Children's programming or nursery care is available.

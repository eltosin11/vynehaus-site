What to expect on a first visit

St. Andrew Kim Korean Catholic Church describes itself: “Partners in the Gospel 을 하고 있는 시애틀 대교구와.” Mass follows the familiar Catholic rhythm of readings, homily, and Eucharist. Visitors are welcome to participate as they're comfortable; non-Catholics typically remain seated or come forward for a blessing during Communion. Children's programming or nursery care is available.

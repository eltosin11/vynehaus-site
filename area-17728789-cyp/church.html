What to expect on a first visit

Εκκλησία Αγίου Κυριακού του Αναχωρητού describes itself: “Church of Panagia Aggeloktisti.” Expect ancient liturgy engaging all the senses — chant, incense, and icons. Much of the congregation stands, and people come and go quietly during the long service; arriving mid-liturgy is normal and unremarkable. Music is chanted liturgy, largely a cappella.

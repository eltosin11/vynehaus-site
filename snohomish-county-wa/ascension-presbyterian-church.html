Ascension Presbyterian Church

8224 220th Street Southwest, Edmonds

What to expect on a first visit

Ascension Presbyterian Church describes itself: “We are a worshipping community of people — from different backgrounds, in different seasons of life, but united in Christ.” The service is orderly and word-centered: hymns, prayers, scripture, and a thoughtful sermon at its heart. Children's programming or nursery care is available. If you'd rather look before you visit, services are streamed online.

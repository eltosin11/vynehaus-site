What to expect on a first visit

All Souls' Anglican Episcopal Church describes itself: “All Souls Anglican Episcopal Church of Chatan is an English-speaking congregation, in Chatan, in the heart of Okinawa Japan.” Worship follows the Book of Common Prayer — a printed order guides you through when to stand, sit, and kneel, so first-timers are never lost. Expect hymns with choir or organ.

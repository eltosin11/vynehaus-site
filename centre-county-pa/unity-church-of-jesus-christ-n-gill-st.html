What to expect on a first visit

Unity Church of Jesus Christ describes itself: “Service Times & Details.” Expect a casual, come-as-you-are atmosphere: a music set, a practical message, and no assumed background knowledge. Signage and greeters usually make first visits easy. Music is led by a contemporary band. Children's programming or nursery care is available.

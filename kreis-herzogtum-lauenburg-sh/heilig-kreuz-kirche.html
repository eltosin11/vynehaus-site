What to expect on a first visit

Heilig-Kreuz-Kirche describes itself: “Wir sind in allen Lebensphasen an Ihrer Seite.” Mass follows the familiar Catholic rhythm of readings, homily, and Eucharist. Visitors are welcome to participate as they're comfortable; non-Catholics typically remain seated or come forward for a blessing during Communion.

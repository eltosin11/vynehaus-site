A documentary about how the world gathers.

We are filming the countless ways people worship — across traditions, countries and cultures. The reverent and the raucous, the ancient and the brand-new.

The film lets each tradition describe itself in its own words — the same principle the directory is built on.

Not finished yet. There is no release date to give you, and we would rather say so than invent one.

What to expect on a first visit

Saint Timothy Episcopal Church describes itself: “Explore the questions you’re asking.” Worship follows the Book of Common Prayer — a printed order guides you through when to stand, sit, and kneel, so first-timers are never lost. Expect hymns with choir or organ.

What to expect on a first visit

Dorfkirche Wallroda describes itself: “Eine sehr gute Zusammenstellung von Informationen über die Dorfkirche finden Sie in diesem Wikipedia-Artikel.” The service follows a printed liturgy with hymns, readings, a sermon, and often Communion. Everything you need to follow along is in the bulletin or on screen. If you'd rather look before you visit, services are streamed online.

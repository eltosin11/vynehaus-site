For churches

Your congregation is probably already listed. Here is what we say about you, and how to change it.

How did we get your details?

From OpenStreetMap and Overture Maps, two open datasets anyone can inspect, and from your own website or social page.

What do you publish about us?

Your name, address, phone and website. Service times, languages, step-free access and a children's programme — each one only where you have said so yourself. And one sentence quoted from your own page.

Do you describe our beliefs?

Never. We quote you, or we say nothing.

Something is wrong. How do we fix it?

Write to us — every church page has a link, and it reaches a person. Or state it plainly on your own site and our next read will pick it up.

Write to us about your listing

We are not listed.

Send your name, address and website. We will add you.

We would rather not be listed.

Ask, and we will remove your page.

What does it cost?

Nothing, and it always will. No premium placement — money moves no church up a page.

Can people review us?

No. No stars, no comments, no rankings. A church is not a restaurant.

Do you favour any tradition?

No. Every tradition is described in the words it uses for itself.

Do you read our website?

Yes, as an identified bot that obeys your robots.txt and waits as long as it asks between requests. Say so there and we stop.

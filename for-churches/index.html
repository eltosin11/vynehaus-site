For churches

Your congregation is probably already listed. Here is exactly what we say about you, where it came from, and how to change it.

What we publish

What is on your page

Only facts, and only ones you have put in public yourself.

Where you are and how to reach you

Name, address, a point on the map, and a phone number and website where we have them.

When you meet

Service times, where your own site states them.

What a visitor needs to know

Languages a service is held in, step-free access, a children's programme, whether a service is streamed — each one only when your own site says so.

A line in your own words

A single sentence taken from your own homepage, quoted and attributed to you. We never write a description of your church, and we never guess at your beliefs. If you have not said it, we do not show it.

Where it comes from

How you came to be listed

Three public sources, no form for you to fill in.

Locations and details come from OpenStreetMap and from Overture Maps, both open datasets that anyone can inspect. Everything else is read from your own website.

When we read your site we identify ourselves as a bot, we obey your robots.txt, and we wait as long as it asks us to between requests. If you would rather we did not read it at all, say so in robots.txt and we will stop.

We do not take anything from Google, from social media pages, or from other church directories.
Corrections

Changing what we say

There is no account to create and nothing to pay.

Every church page carries a link to report a correction, and it reaches a person. Tell us what is wrong and what it should say. The quickest fix of all is to state the thing plainly on your own site — our next read will pick it up.

Write to us about your listing

If you are not listed

Send us your name, address and website and we will add you.

If you would rather not be listed

Ask, and we will remove your page.

Our promise

What we will never do

Charge you

Listing is free and will stay free. There is no premium placement, and no amount of money moves a church up a page.

Let people review you

No star ratings, no comments, no rankings. A church is not a restaurant.

Favour a tradition

Catholic, Pentecostal, Orthodox, Quaker — every tradition is described on its own terms, in the words that tradition uses for itself.

Speak for you

We do not characterise your doctrine, your politics or your welcome. We quote you, or we say nothing.

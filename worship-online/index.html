Worship online

Four countries are missing from this directory on purpose. If you are in one of them, these are services in your own language that you can watch from home.

Why those countries are missing

In China we list the registered churches. The congregations that are not registered are not here, and we do not go looking for them — not in any dataset, not on any map, not anywhere. Iran, Saudi Arabia and Yemen are left out altogether.

An entry in a directory is a published address. In these four countries the address is the part that can be used against a congregation, so we do not hold it.

What we can tell you, and what we cannot

We can tell you who streams, in what language, and at what hour. We cannot tell you whether watching is safe where you are. We do not know your situation, your connection or what the law says where you live this week, and we are not going to guess.

This page is plain HTML. It loads nothing from anywhere else and records nothing about you. The clock below is worked out by your own browser and is not sent anywhere.

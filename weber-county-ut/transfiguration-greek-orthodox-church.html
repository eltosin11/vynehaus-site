Transfiguration Greek Orthodox Church

674 42nd Street, South Ogden

What to expect on a first visit

Transfiguration Greek Orthodox Church describes itself: “Transfiguration Greek Orthodox Church, an Orthodox Christian community serving Ogden and Northern Utah.” Expect ancient liturgy engaging all the senses — chant, incense, and icons. Much of the congregation stands, and people come and go quietly during the long service; arriving mid-liturgy is normal and unremarkable. Music is chanted liturgy, largely a cappella. If you'd rather look before you visit, services are streamed online.

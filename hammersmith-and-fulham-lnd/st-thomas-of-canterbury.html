What to expect on a first visit

St Thomas of Canterbury describes itself: “Laudetur Iesus Christus – Semper laudetur!” Mass follows the familiar Catholic rhythm of readings, homily, and Eucharist. Visitors are welcome to participate as they're comfortable; non-Catholics typically remain seated or come forward for a blessing during Communion. Expect hymns with choir or organ. If you'd rather look before you visit, services are streamed online.

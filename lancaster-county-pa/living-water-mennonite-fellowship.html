What to expect on a first visit

Living Water Mennonite Fellowship describes itself: “Living Water Mennonite Fellowship Visit Us Live Stream Welcome to Living Water Mennonite Fellowship.” The service centers on the sermon, with a relaxed structure around it. Expect hymns with choir or organ. Children's programming or nursery care is available. If you'd rather look before you visit, services are streamed online.

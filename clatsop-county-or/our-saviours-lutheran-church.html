What to expect on a first visit

Our Saviours Lutheran Church describes itself: “Our Lady of Victory & St. Peter The Fisherman.” The service follows a printed liturgy with hymns, readings, a sermon, and often Communion. Everything you need to follow along is in the bulletin or on screen.

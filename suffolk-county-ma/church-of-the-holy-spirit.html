Church of the Holy Spirit

525 River Street, Mattapan

What to expect on a first visit

Church of the Holy Spirit describes itself: “We are an Episcopal church located in the heart of Mattapan, MA.” Worship follows the Book of Common Prayer — a printed order guides you through when to stand, sit, and kneel, so first-timers are never lost. Expect hymns with choir or organ. Children's programming or nursery care is available. If you'd rather look before you visit, services are streamed online.

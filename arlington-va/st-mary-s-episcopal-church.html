Their story

“Tradition done well” — thoughtful liturgy and strong preaching, with an openly affirming, inclusive community.

What to expect on a first visit

First-time visitors to St. Mary's Episcopal Church will experience a liturgy-centered service with traditional music in English. The church welcomes newcomers and openly affirms LGBTQ+ individuals. Women hold leadership roles in the church, and the service times are on Sundays at 8:00 and 10:15 am.

Their story

“A church for people who don't go to church.” Built for the curious and the cautious.

What to expect on a first visit

Grace Community Church describes itself: “A Church For People Who Don't Go To Church.” First-time visitors can expect a preaching-centered service with contemporary music in English and Spanish. Services are held on Sundays at 9:00, 10:15, and 11:30am. The church welcomes newcomers and offers childcare, livestream options, and parking. A coffee bar is available after the service.

Their story

Vibrant Pentecostal worship and a cultural anchor for the Nigerian diaspora. Monthly Holy Ghost vigils.

What to expect on a first visit

First-time visitors can expect a spirit-led worship experience with high-energy gospel and contemporary music in English. The church welcomes seekers and offers practical support for kids. Services are held on Sundays at 9:00 AM and include monthly all-night vigils.

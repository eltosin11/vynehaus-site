What to expect on a first visit

Ardsley Bible Chapel describes itself: “Just another WordPress site.” Expect a casual, come-as-you-are atmosphere: a music set, a practical message, and no assumed background knowledge. Signage and greeters usually make first visits easy. Expect hymns with choir or organ. Children's programming or nursery care is available.

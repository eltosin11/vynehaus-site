Lutheran Church of the Resurrection

420 Stewart Ave, Garden City

What to expect on a first visit

The service follows a printed liturgy with hymns, readings, a sermon, and often Communion. Everything you need to follow along is in the bulletin or on screen. Music is led by a contemporary band. Children's programming or nursery care is available. If you'd rather look before you visit, services are streamed online.

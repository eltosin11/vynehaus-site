Christ Episcopal Church

369 Sand Shore Road, Budd Lake

What to expect on a first visit

Christ Episcopal Church describes itself: “"Proclaiming God By Serving God's Children".” Worship follows the Book of Common Prayer — a printed order guides you through when to stand, sit, and kneel, so first-timers are never lost. Expect hymns with choir or organ. Children's programming or nursery care is available.
